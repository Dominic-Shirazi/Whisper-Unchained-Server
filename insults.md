# Insults

A curated bank of witty, condescending one-liners ("insults so smart they take a
few minutes to land"). They are grouped by the **reasoning failure the recipient
is making** so the AI can pick one that actually fits the argument it is
responding to, instead of grabbing a random line.

How the AI should use this file:

- Read the user's brain-dump and work out _what the other person got wrong_ (are
  they confidently wrong? ignoring evidence? posing as an expert? just slow?).
- Pick the category below that matches that flaw, then weave in at least ONE line from it, multiple if it's longer and it makes sense to.
- You may reword an insult, or invent a similar one in the same spirit, so it
  flows naturally with the user's voice. Keep the sting subtle.
- Where a line has a `(...)` note, follow it (e.g. swap in a word from their text).

## Confidently wrong / overconfident

- I have never been so impressed with anyone's ability to be this wrong.
- You have the confidence of someone who's never Googled themselves.
- You have the confidence of someone who's never used Google before.
- I am honored to witness your impressive lack of understanding.
- Your ignorance is an absolute triumph of human existence.
- When someone says "do you know who I am?" — no, but I bet those who do are envious of me.

## Ignores evidence / allergic to facts

- I admire how you never let evidence interfere with your opinions.
- Don't let my knowledge get in the way of your confidence.
- You have a rare talent for speaking at length without disturbing the facts.
- You're not wrong, you're just operating in a different intellectual timezone.

## Fakes expertise / unearned authority

- You've mastered the tone of authority without the inconvenience of depth.
- It's interesting what you've done with your education.
- As an outsider, what are your views on intelligence?
- You're the reason instructions have drawings.

## Just not very bright / slow on the uptake

- I refuse to engage in a battle of wits with an unarmed man.
- I can explain it to you, but I can't understand it for you.
- I could explain it to you, but I left my crayons at home.
- Light travels faster than sound. That's why some people appear bright until you hear them speak.
- When it comes to thinking, you travel light.
- It's impossible to underestimate you.
- Intelligence pursues you, but you appear to be a master of evasion.
- You're clearly intelligent, it just doesn't show up where it matters.
- It's truly remarkable that you are the strongest swimmer your father had.
- You absolute WiFi-dependent potato. (swap "WiFi-dependent" for something pulled from their text)
- You ain't the dumbest guy in the world, but you'd better hope he doesn't die.
- I bet you're the kind of person who'd climb over a glass wall to see what's on the other side.

## Closed-minded / refuses to learn

- I can see you are committed to your current level of understanding.
- I can see why that makes sense to you.
- I love how you think outside the box; it's clear no one's ever invited in.
- I wish I didn't have common sense. You seem so happy.

## Lazy / dead weight at work

- I know you're not afraid of hard work. I've seen you stand near it all day.
- Don't just stand there, put your hands in your pockets too.
- Do you ever feel guilty cashing your paycheck?
- How long are you planning to be new at this?
- You're like a rusty rifle: you won't work, and you can't be fired.
- You do what you can, and I'll do what you can't.

## Adds nothing / wasted everyone's time

- Thank you for helping me. It was like doing it myself, only harder.
- You have a unique way of contributing without adding much.
- Your food for thought leaves everyone hungry.
- I admire your courage to speak in the absence of knowledge.
- If you're not going to do anything, at least clap when I do something.

## Just unpleasant to be around

- I'm jealous of everyone that hasn't met you.
- Nothing lights up a room like your absence.
- You absolutely light up every room you walk out of.
- You have a special way of speaking that makes people appreciate your silence.
- You think your parents ever get tired of waiting to be proud of you?
